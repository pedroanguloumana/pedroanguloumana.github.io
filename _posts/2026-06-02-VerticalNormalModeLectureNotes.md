I gave a guest lecture for the graduate tropical meteorology course at SNU on the subject of the vertical normal modes (VNMs) of the atmosphere. The notes are a mostly self-contained guide on what the VNMs are, how their forms can be derived in the case of constant stratification (i.e., $\partial_z N^2=0$), and how the VNMs can be computed numerically from data.

The notes are available as a [PDF download](/assets/docs/vertical_normal_modes.pdf). A complementary [Python notebook](https://github.com/pedroanguloumana/Vertical_Normal_Modes_Notes/blob/master/Computing_Vertical_Normal_Modes.ipynb) demonstrates the numerical computation, and can also be opened directly in [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/pedroanguloumana/Vertical_Normal_Modes_Notes/blob/master/Computing_Vertical_Normal_Modes.ipynb).
